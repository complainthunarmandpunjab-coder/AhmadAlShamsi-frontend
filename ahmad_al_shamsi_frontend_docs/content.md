# Content and Editorial Guidelines

## Voice
- Confident, informed, calm, helpful, and internationally understandable.
- Write like an experienced property advisor, not a sales script.
- Prefer specific, verifiable information over vague superlatives.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, excessive exclamation marks, and fake urgency.

## Content accuracy
- The reference screenshot shows sample names, prices, property facts, reviews, and a contact number. Treat all such values as illustrative until confirmed by the business.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, property inventory, prices, phone numbers, addresses, awards, licenses, developer partnerships, transaction volumes, or claims of market leadership.
- Clearly mark unpublished draft content internally and prevent it from being indexed.
- Property prices and availability must be updated from a reliable source and display an appropriate last-updated timestamp when practical.
- Use approved legal text for privacy, cookie, and terms pages.

## Image and media
- Use licensed, owner-provided, or properly licensed photography.
- Keep a consistent warm-neutral photo grade but do not over-edit property imagery in ways that misrepresent the property.
- Provide captions when they add useful context.
- Do not use generated imagery as evidence of a real listed property. Generated waterfront scenes may be used as decorative mood imagery only when clearly not presented as an actual property listing.
